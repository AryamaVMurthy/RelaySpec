# Current research priorities

The campaign seeks reusable findings and applications, not the largest development speed number. Each new direction needs a controlled comparison and an explicit reason to continue.

1. **Compact conditioning interface — primary new evidence.** Two late layers retain 97.63% [96.23, 99.03]% throughput with 60% fewer mapper parameters on 64 separately frozen requests. This establishes a practical compact interface in the tested DFlash setting. Mapper arithmetic is below 1% of request time, so fewer parameters should not be sold as a large latency gain.
2. **Native application — confirmed secondary evidence.** Native rank-1536 projection compression retains 98.66% [97.58, 99.75]% versus released native with 55% fewer projection parameters. Native two-layer distillation now passes a separate frozen64-question comparison:97.05%[95.93,98.21]% retention with60% fewer projection parameters; one layer retains84.55%. All five methods60/64correct with identical outputs and no capped answers. SVD and interface distillation are not claimed as new general compression algorithms.
3. **Calibration composition — replicated development insight.** The retargeted dialogue gain repeats at both fitting seeds on sixteen additional conversations: about 7.5%, compared with 0.3–1.1% for native interfaces. Similar proportional feature-error reductions have different decoding effects. Other initial workloads have intervals including parity. No dialogue-quality claim is available.
4. **Downward retargeting — applicability boundary passed in development.** The 4B-trained drafter works with the 0.6B verifier after 512-record fitting, giving 3.26× AR throughput on sixteen completed GSM8K answers and gains on code/dialogue screens. AR and speculative outputs can differ in BF16. This is an applicability example, not a fresh confirmation or recommendation about small-verifier quality.
5. **Numerical agreement — supporting evaluation rigor.** Full FP32 gives within-runtime AR agreement on all tested 0.6B math/code/dialogue cases and sixteen 8B questions. Numerical sensitivity and FP32 mitigation are established prior work (Yuan et al., 2506.09501). Head-only FP32 improves initial 8B agreement to 16/16 but only 9/16 for 0.6B and does not reproduce full-FP32 AR. Wave29 completed: head-only agreement improves15/32→20/32 GSM8K and3/16→5/16 MATH, equally for native and RelaySpec. Native/relay sequences match in every arm. Close this as a partial mitigation, not a general repair. Do not let this supporting diagnostic displace the interface contribution or become an indefinite precision sweep.
6. **Early-layer nonlinearity — closed negative direction.** Parameter-matched MLP training fits features better but does not close the large early/late decoding gap. No additional width sweep is justified by the completed eight-question screen.
7. **Compression objective — retain the family-dependent result.** EAGLE benefits much more from fitting activation outputs than preserving weights. Direct fitting remains competitive. Cite ASVD/SVD-LLM and keep unequal total adaptation budgets explicit.
