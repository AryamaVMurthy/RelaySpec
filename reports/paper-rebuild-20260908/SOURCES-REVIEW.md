## Decisions for the main paper

The defensible contribution is **retargeting the conditioning interface of a released feature-conditioned drafter, keeping its prediction network fixed, and removing its original source transformer from generation**. The empirical contribution is the cost/performance regime and its limits: small calibration sets often suffice, greater nonlinear capacity need not improve decoding, and cross-family transfer can benefit from a different data/optimization recipe. A linear connector by itself, frozen-drafter adaptation, reuse across targets, cross-vocabulary speculation, and late-layer selection each have prior art.

Keep closest alternatives prominent without turning the paper into an unsupported universal ranking. A compact mechanism table should separate (i) which inherited object is reused, (ii) weights trained for a new target, (iii) retained model execution, and (iv) target verification. A quantitative table can report the completed public-runtime PARD and frozen SD² arms, explicitly retaining their own AR controls and precision/runtime differences. PARD-2 and TriSpec have not been evaluated here; do not imply otherwise.

The strongest comparison of adaptation mechanisms remains the matched-runtime feature-loss versus connector-CE/LoRA study. Public-runtime throughput is deployment evidence, not a causal estimate of the mapper's advantage over every competing algorithm. The paper can say RelaySpec has the highest observed throughput among those tested configurations, but not that it beats the full versions of all methods.

## Contradictions to repair in the rewritten text

- Replace the unqualified shared-vocabulary scope in related work and conclusion. The original Qwen studies use a shared vocabulary; the new Qwen-to-Llama study uses an explicitly limited bridge. Describe the exact common-token/byte alignment and reject/fallback behavior from the implementation rather than implying unrestricted tokenizer interchangeability.
- Replace an exclusively “larger target” story. The new Llama source is Llama-3.1-8B and its target is Llama-3.2-3B: this is downscaling within a family. A general “new target” framing unifies upgrade, downgrade, and cross-family transfer.
- Keep the original Qwen precision and quality evidence separate from the new FP32 target result. The 128 exact matches per arm do not retroactively certify every old Qwen/PARD/SD² run, and exact sequence matching is not mathematical-answer accuracy.
- Replace universal data saturation language. The new selected cross-family 8,192-record recipe improves over its historical 4,096-record baseline, whereas the 16,384-record Llama candidate ties the cheaper historical fit. Data amount and optimization changed together in the selected comparison; do not attribute the entire gain solely to sample count.
- Broaden the AI-use statement to accurately cover hypothesis/experiment design and result interpretation, in addition to coding and editing. Those tasks are visible in the session record. Do not fabricate a completed human review or named-author verification process.

## Official ICLR 2027 requirements

The [official author guidelines](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AuthorGuidelines) specify **at most nine main-text pages for initial submission**. References are excluded; appendices follow references and have no page limit. The later rebuttal/camera-ready allowance is ten pages. A stray camera-ready sentence calls ten pages identical to submission, but the initial-submission rule and official template both explicitly say nine.

The [official AI policy](https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2027/AIPolicyForAuthors) requires an AI-use section outside the page count and disclosure in the submission form. Ethics and reproducibility statements are recommended and also excluded. Anonymous submission applies to text and supplements. The template limits AI and ethics statements to one page each; a brief reproducibility paragraph should point to protocols, code and artifacts.

Downloaded the [official style archive](https://media.iclr.cc/Conferences/ICLR2027/iclr-2027-style-files.zip). All local conference `.sty`/`.bst` and bundled dependency style files match its bytes. See `official-template-check.txt` for hashes. Do not change margins or shrink template geometry to fit. Verify the main-text endpoint in the rendered PDF, then inspect every main page for clipped figures/tables and unsupported claims.
